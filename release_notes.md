## 🇺🇸 English

### ✨ Key Features:
- ⏱️ **Live Home Screen Widgets**: Real-time second-by-second countdown (`DAYS : HOURS : MIN/SEC`) powered by native Chronometer with zero battery drain.
- 🎮 **Game Coverage**: Track start dates, progress bars, and live statuses for all major ARPG & MMO games.
- 🎨 **Customization**: Adjust widget transparency, background themes, and card layouts.
- 🔔 **Custom Reminders**: Set notifications for 1 week, 3 days, 24 hours, 1 hour before launch, or right at start time.
- 📊 **Stability**: Integrated Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics for reliability.

### 🛡️ Notice about Google Play Protect / Installation Warning:
> **Why Play Protect might warn you:**
> Since SeasonForge is distributed directly as an APK via GitHub (outside the Google Play Store), Google Play Protect may show an "Unrecognized Developer" or "Block by Play Protect" warning.
>
> **How to install safely:**
> 1. Tap **"More details"** (or *Подробнее*).
> 2. Tap **"Install anyway"** (or *Всё равно установить*).
